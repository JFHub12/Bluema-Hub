01

Data Controller & Corporate Identity

This Privacy Policy governs the processing of personal information by BLUEMA HUB ("BLUEMA", "we", "us", or "our"), operating in Accra, Republic of Ghana. We act as the sole Data Controller for all transactions performed via our web application and mobile Progressive Web App (PWA).

We process your personal information strictly in accordance with the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and modern global consumer data privacy principles.

02

Categories of Personal Data Collected

To deliver authenticated goods, honor equipment warranties, and protect against counterfeit fraud, we collect only essential information:

A. Account & Contact Details

Full legal name, active telephone number (for dispatch calls and Mobile Money authorization), and email address (for account recovery, OTP tokens, and receipts).

B. Delivery & Geospatial Coordinates

Ghana Post Digital GPS Address (e.g. GA-123-4567), street name, building landmark, and metropolitan city to dispatch our vetted white-glove couriers.

C. Transaction & Order Logs

Order identification numbers, item SKU specifications, selected storage tiers, apparel sizing, and transaction timestamps for warranty registration.

D. Technical Session Tokens

Anonymized browser session keys to sustain your shopping bag items, theme preferences, and offline PWA service worker caching.

03

Purpose & Lawful Basis of Processing

We process your personal information solely for legitimate contractual and service purposes:

  • Fulfillment of Purchase Contract: Preparing, packaging, and dispatching orders to your verified physical address.
  • Mandatory Customer Authentication: Verifying buyer identity prior to checkout to protect couriers, eliminate unauthorized card usage, and bind warranties to legitimate users.
  • Hardware Warranty Protection: Recording screen batch serials and iPhone IMEIs against your account for 7-day screen test windows and manufacturer claims.
  • Customer Concierge Support: Responding via telephone or WhatsApp when you request technical guidance or delivery status updates.
04

Payment Data Isolation & Financial Security

Zero Storage of Sensitive Financial Credentials:

BLUEMA HUB servers do NOT store raw credit card CVVs, card PINs, or Mobile Money secret PINs. All payment transactions utilize tokenized, end-to-end encrypted merchant APIs.

When authorizing a Mobile Money transaction (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, or AT Money), the transaction payload triggers a secure telecom network prompt on your physical handset. Your authorization PIN is entered solely within the encrypted SIM/USSD environment of your telecommunications operator.

05

Vetted Courier Disclosure & Minimal Sharing

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers or external brokers. Disclosure occurs strictly on a need-to-know basis:

  • Assigned Delivery Rider: Receives only your recipient name, contact phone number, and delivery landmark to locate you.
  • Regional Couriers (Intercity): Dispatched with physical parcel tracking waybills across Kumasi, Takoradi, and regional depots.
  • Law Enforcement & Fraud Authorities: Disclosed only if mandated by valid court subpoena or formal investigation into stolen hardware.
06

Hardware Serial & IMEI Verification Logging

For high-value Apple replacement screens and Apple iPhones:

We record device IMEI numbers and GX OLED factory serial batches to protect customers against counterfeit substitution and honor genuine warranty claims. This diagnostic logging ensures that if a device requires servicing or exchange, its authenticity can be validated immediately against your order invoice.

07

Local Storage, Cookies & Offline PWA Caching

Our web application uses essential client-side storage technologies:

  • Shopping Bag Persistence: Preserves your selected merchandise across browser refreshes so your cart remains intact.
  • Service Worker (sw.js): Enables offline browsing of product specs and catalogs in our Progressive Web App (PWA) during poor cellular signal.
  • Authentication Tokens: Maintains your verified login state securely to eliminate repetitive password prompts.
08

Your Rights & Account Information Removal

As a registered customer, you hold comprehensive rights over your personal data:

  • Access & Inspection: You may inspect all personal information registered in your customer account.
  • Correction: You may update your delivery address, phone number, or name through your profile modal or customer concierge.
  • Account Deletion: You may request complete erasure of your member profile and contact coordinates by contacting our Data Protection Concierge. (Order invoices with statutory tax or warranty obligations will be archived in compliance with Ghanaian fiscal laws).
