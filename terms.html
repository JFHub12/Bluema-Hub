03

Apple Parts Testing & Installation Protocol

Mandatory Dry-Testing Notice for Technicians and Buyers:

All iPhone OLED screens, battery replacements, and display modules must be dry-tested (connecting flex cables to motherboard without removing plastic screen protectors or applying adhesive glue) prior to physical mounting.

Once a replacement screen or battery has had its manufacturer warranty seal broken, adhesive tape applied, or frame glued down, it cannot be returned under our replacement policy, as improper installation pressure or adhesive solvents invalidate manufacturer warranties.

  • Testing Window: Buyers and technicians have a 7-day inspection window from courier delivery to test screen responsiveness, TrueTone transfer, and touch sensitivity.
  • Defect Reporting: In the rare event of a display anomaly during dry-test, contact our Concierge Hotline (059 883 0932) with order number and video evidence.

Certified Apple iPhones Warranty Guarantee

All Apple iPhones sold through BLUEMA HUB are certified genuine:

Pricing, Payment Gateways & Mobile Money

All catalog prices are listed in Ghanaian Cedis (GH₵). We offer three verified transaction channels:

3. Pay on Delivery (Accra Metropolitan Zone Only): Available for vetted member orders. You inspect your goods or phone packaging upon courier arrival prior to making immediate Mobile Money or cash payment.
